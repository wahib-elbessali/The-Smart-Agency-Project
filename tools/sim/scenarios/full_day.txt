# A full working day at a branch, from an empty app to closing time.
#
#   python tools/sim/play.py tools/sim/scenarios/full_day.txt
#   python tools/sim/play.py tools/sim/scenarios/full_day.txt --from "Opening"     (resume at a section)
#
# Every part can be simulated or real: "python tools/sim/w.py real" shows the
# choice, "w.py real kiosk on" makes the kiosk the real board. A step played by a
# real part tells you what to do in real life instead (the text after ">>").
# The day's times are applied when the backend runs on the fake clock, and only
# shown when it runs on the real clock (needed as soon as anything is real).

section Setup 1: the stack
say Start everything (docs/mocking-guide.md part 2.2). Memory-hungry on a laptop: close what you do not need.
do Docker: Postgres, Redis and the MQTT broker are running
do The AI service runs: cd ai && uvicorn features.main:app --port 8001
do The dashboard runs on the real backend: frontend/.env.local has VITE_USE_MOCKS=false, then cd frontend && npx vite --config vite.sim.config.ts
say Choose now what is simulated and what is real (all simulated by default), in another terminal:
say   python tools/sim/w.py real                    shows the choice
say   python tools/sim/w.py real kiosk on           the real kiosk board (same for gate, display, door)
say   python tools/sim/w.py real cameras on         real cameras watched by the real AI service
say Anything real needs the backend on the REAL clock (real boards and cameras stamp real time).
do Your choice is set (python tools/sim/w.py real)
do The backend runs. All simulated: AI_SERVICE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8123 in backend/.env, then python tools/sim/run_fake_clock.py 2026-10-26T07:45:00Z. Real cameras: AI_SERVICE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8001 and uvicorn app.main:app

section Setup 2: the branch, in the app
say Sign in as the admin. Everything below is done in the dashboard, like on a real branch.
do Agencies: add the branch, open 08:30, close 16:30
do Services: Virement (code VIR) and Credit (code CRE)
do The branch's page: counters Guichet 1 (Virement) and Guichet 2 (Credit for now)
do Employees: Security, Agent and Manager, with the badges RFID-DEV-SECURITY, RFID-DEV-AGENT and RFID-DEV-MANAGER (real gate: the badge numbers of your real cards, and change them below too)
do The branch's page, rooms: "Local technique" (Security and Manager only, not the Agent) and "Bureau de direction" (Manager only)
do Users: an Agent account and a Manager account for this branch (the counters are served from their screens)

section Setup 3: the boards
say Simulated boards are registered by a script; real ones by you.
do Simulated boards: python tools/sim/setup_sim.py devices --email ADMIN_EMAIL --password ADMIN_PASSWORD (add --mqtt-port if your broker is not on 1883)
do Real boards: on the Devices page, add each real one with its board id (ticket-kiosk-01, rfid-gate-01, queue-display-01, access-sensors-1), flash its key and power it on. Check it shows online
do The world runs (it plays whatever is simulated): cd tools/sim && uvicorn world:app --port 8123
do Badge rooms for the script: python tools/sim/setup_sim.py rooms --email ADMIN_EMAIL --password ADMIN_PASSWORD

section Setup 4: the cameras
say Simulated cameras: the world films a 12 m x 8 m floor. Register them under these exact names.
say Real cameras: your own names and stream URLs; place the calibration rectangle and markers on the real floor.
do Cameras page: add cam1 (entrance wall) and cam2 (side wall): people tracking, weapon, fire
do Cameras page: add cam3 (kiosk camera, faces): watch list and satisfaction
do Cameras page: add cam4 (director's office door camera): no detections, used for badge + face doors
say Stream URLs for the simulated ones: rtsp://127.0.0.1:8554/cam1 ... cam4 (the world answers for them by name)
do Calibration: calibrate cam1 by clicking the yellow rectangle's corners R1, R2, R3, R4 in order (simulated: 1.5 m x 1 m)
do Calibration: calibrate cam2 the same way, on the same rectangle
do Calibration: align cam1 and cam2 by clicking the same pink markers (M1 to M6) in both views
do Tracking: use cam1 and cam2 for people tracking. The Live map should say tracking is running
do Zones: draw Gate (at the entrance), Kiosk zone (in front of BORNE), Waiting area (ATTENTE), Counter 1 desk zone (behind GUICHET 1) and Counter 2 desk zone (behind GUICHET 2)
do The branch's page: kiosk zone = Kiosk zone, kiosk face camera = cam3
do Devices page: the gate reader's badge zone = Gate
do Staffing: workstations Guichet 1 (Counter 1 desk zone) and Guichet 2 (Counter 2 desk zone), each linked to its counter
say Shortcut, simulated cameras only: python tools/sim/setup_sim.py vision does all of Setup 4 by itself.
say Setup done. Open the Live map, Employee presence and the Visitor queue side by side.
pause
